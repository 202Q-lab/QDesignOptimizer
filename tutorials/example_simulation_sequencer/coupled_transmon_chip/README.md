# Two coupled transmons with readout and charge lines

Two transmons at 4 and 5 GHz, each with a readout resonator (6 and
7 GHz, 1 MHz linewidth) and a charge line that may limit T1 to no less
than 20 ms, coupled through a resonator at 9 GHz. Quick capacitance
studies tune each resonator's linewidth and each charge line, then an
eigenmode solve tunes the qubit with its resonator.

The chip is the one of `examples_coupled_transmon_chip`. A report takes
this file's heading as the design's name and its first paragraph as its
purpose.
