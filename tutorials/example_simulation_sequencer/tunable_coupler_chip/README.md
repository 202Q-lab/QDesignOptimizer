# Two transmons with a tunable coupler

Two fixed-frequency transmons at 4.16 and 4.00 GHz, each read out by its
own resonator (7.12 and 7.07 GHz), coupled through a tunable-coupler
transmon at 5.45 GHz. All five modes are tuned together in one
eigenmode solve: frequencies, anharmonicities, and the qubit–resonator
and coupler–qubit dispersive shifts.

The chip is the one of `examples_coupled_transmons_tunable_coupler_chip`,
as `main_eigenmode_two_qubit_resonator.ipynb` tunes it. A report takes
this file's heading as the design's name and its first paragraph as its
purpose.
